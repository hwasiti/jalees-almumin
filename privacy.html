Privacy Policy

Jalees al-Mumin (جليس المؤمن) · Android app, application id com.jalees.almumin · Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short

The app has no accounts, no developer server, no advertising and no analytics. What you save stays on your phone. The developer never receives your data. The only data that leaves your phone is what you choose to send to your own Google Drive (the optional backup), and the requests described below that some optional features make to third-party services.

What the app stores on your phone

Your chosen city and its coordinates (typed, or from the device's GPS if you allow location), prayer and calendar settings, reminders, bookmarks, notes and marks you write, ticks, make-up (qada) counters, reading positions and listening history. This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not shared with the developer.

Optional: Google Drive backup

If you turn on the Drive backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission lets the app see and manage only the files the app itself created in your Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • What is written: a backup file (JSON) holding the data listed above (including your saved place and its coordinates, your notes, reminders and listening history, and the phone's model). It is not encrypted.
  • Where it goes: directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. It does not pass through any server of the developer, and the developer cannot read it.
  • What the app does with it: it only lets you restore your data to this or another phone, and keeps a small number of recent backups.
  • Your control: you can turn the backup off, disconnect the account, or delete the backup files from your Drive at any time, and you can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional: backup to a file

You may also save the same backup to a file in a place you choose on your phone. That file is yours; the app does not send it anywhere.

Requests to third-party services

These happen only when you use the feature, and the app sends nothing else:

  • City search and place detection: the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service; to detect an approximate place, your IP address is seen by ipwho.is or ipapi.co.
  • Audio recitations: streaming or downloading a recording fetches it from shiavoice.com.
  • Flight mode (beta, optional): flight lookups use OpenSky Network and, if you enter your own key, FlightAware AeroAPI. The key stays on your phone, outside the backup.
  • Dictionary: choosing to look up a word opens almaany.com in your browser.

Those services have their own privacy policies. The app itself does not include advertising or analytics libraries.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
